Rising from the Florida swamps...it's the Dukes of Dacron, the Rastas of Rayon, the Princes of Polyester, the most flammable band in Florida...the LOVEGODS IN LEISURE SUITS.

May 16, 2026: Club Space Fish was a huge success! After a mere 34 years, the Lovegods returned to the stage for one unforgettable night. With a lineup featuring original members Michael Bales, Tom Arline, Kevin Berve, and Rich Thibault, as well as "new guys" Robert Wainwright, Jason Ackerman, and Sean Cote, the band delivered a high-energy set that spanned their entire catalog.

Thank you to everyone who came out to celebrate the show with us!
